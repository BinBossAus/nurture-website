One discipline to add: Rose Gold is for calls to action, focus rings and accent hairlines only. The moment it becomes a background for large blocks it stops reading as precious. Espresso is your only dark field; Ivory is your only light one.

Glass, glow and depth — the Custom CSS to keep

Your .nurture-glass class is good and worth keeping. Three amendments:

  • Add transition: box-shadow .5s, transform .5s and a hover state that lifts translateY(-3px) — glass that never responds reads as a flat panel.
  • Cap the glow. 0 10px 30px rgba(45,36,32,.05) is right; the button's 0 6px 28px rgba(203,177,165,.5) is too strong at 50% — bring it to .28.
  • Add @media (prefers-reduced-motion: reduce) around every transform. Required for accessibility, and some of your readers are reading one-handed at 3am.

For the 3D cover shelf: it needs perspective: 1600px on the container and transform-style: preserve-3d on every wrapper between the container and the covers, or the depth silently flattens. Covers get rotateY(-8deg / -14deg / -19deg) with increasing negative translateZ, and part outward on hover. Working version is in direction 1c.

Part two · page copy

The voice, stated once

Specific over aspirational. Named expertise rather than "world-renowned experts". Australian spelling. No emoji, no "busy bees", no exclamation marks, no "👀". Claims tied to something checkable: 80 pages, bi-monthly, since 2012, 1,000+ newsagents, 40,000 online newsstands, FSC recycled stock, plant-based inks, compostable satchels, one tree per annual subscription. Sentences that end.

Corrections found in your export, now used throughout: Nurture is bi-monthly (6 issues a year), and pricing is $20 single · $55 for 3 · $95 for 6. My earlier homepage mockups showed $45/$75 and "four times a year" — both wrong, and I'll correct the three directions.
