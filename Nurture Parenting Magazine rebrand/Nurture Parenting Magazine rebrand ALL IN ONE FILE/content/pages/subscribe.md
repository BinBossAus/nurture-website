Evidence Based Articles












Our philosophy is to nourish the most important bonds, first with yourself, then with your family and close relationships. We help parents seek out the practical tools and knowledge they need to feel empowered and supported as they child-rear. From pregnancy, to the early learning years, we are there through it all!

Nurture features comprehensive, informative, evidence based articles, giving you an insight into the many styles of natural parenting that exist in today’s world, thus ‘allowing’ parents to take an ‘intuitive’ approach based on their own individual family’s needs.

Fundamentally, Nurture advocates the principles of Attachment Parenting, and the Attachment Theory – A Secure Base for Babies and Parents. Attachment is a deep and enduring emotional bond that connects one person to another, across time and space (Ainsworth, 1973; Bowlby, 1969).

Core Topics


EMOTIONAL DEVELOPMENT












Ensuring that we parent in such a way that fulfills children’s emotional needs so that they can see and model such behaviour. More ME time, less screen time! This is the ultimate parenting magazine that nurtures emotional development, encourages healing and wholeness at all phases of life, and supports the choice to embrace a holistic and perfect paradigm.



The parenting experts featured in Nurture Parenting Magazine, will guide you through raising confident, resilient children who are emotionally secure. There is a wide range of evidence based articles, from a natural parenting prospective to support you on your parenting journey.



















INTELLECTUAL DEVELOPMENT











Helping children have a well-rounded child-led education, which involves not only academics but also creativity to ensure that they can ‘learn at their own individual pace.’ Nurture Parenting magazine is the parenting magazine that helps you raise your children with an unstructured learning environment. It is ideal for home educators and unschoolers.



















PHYSICAL DEVELOPMENT











Nurture Parenting has become one of Australia’s leading magazines, for parents and carers looking for gentle, evidence-based ways to nurture their children. With over 50 regular parenting authors, Nurture takes you on an informative, and friendly journey through pregnancy, birthing & breastfeeding, to the early learning years. Nurture does not support any form of sleep training.



















SPIRITUAL DEVELOPMENT












Nurture Parenting Magazine is a bimonthly periodical that focuses on the spiritual development of children. The magazine offers tools and strategies to help parents raise children that value themselves, others and the environment, by giving them an appreciation for their own needs, feelings and thoughts, as well as other people’s similarities and differences.
