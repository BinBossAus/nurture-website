## From conception to connection


At Nurture, we believe the most important bonds start with yourself—then ripple outward to your children, family, and wider village. Our mission is simple: to support parents with heart-led tools and evidence-based wisdom from pregnancy through to the early learning years.

Since 2012, Nurture has been published quarterly in print and shared across Australia and the world digitally. From 2026, after a break, we're bringing Nurture to families bi-monthly. We’re proud to bring together thought-provoking voices, parenting professionals, and real-life stories that offer insight into gentle, respectful, and holistic parenting styles.



## our why



Nurture is grounded in the principles of Attachment Parenting and Attachment Theory—a secure base that supports both babies and parents. As Bowlby and Ainsworth highlighted decades ago, attachment is the deep emotional bond that connects one person to another across time and space. And we believe that connection is everything.




## our beginnings



Nurture was founded by Kristy Pillinger, a new mum at the time, who discovered that the parenting resources she craved didn’t quite exist—ones that felt both intuitive and evidence-based. With her firstborn William in arms and a heart full of ideas, Kristy created the magazine she wished she had.

From its humble beginnings, Nurture has grown into a trusted voice in the parenting space—led by parents, for parents.




## why it matters



For generations, parenting was shaped by habit rather than research. But now we know that how we parent—especially in those early formative years—can literally shape a child’s brain and nervous system. The way we connect, respond, and nurture affects their lifelong capacity for resilience, self-regulation, and deep relationships.

We carry our own childhood patterns into adulthood. But with awareness, support, and tools, we can parent with presence—not just by default.




## our philosophy



Nourish the most important bonds, first with yourself, then with your family and close relationships. We help parents seek out the practical tools and knowledge they need to feel empowered and supported as they child-rear. From pregnancy to the early learning years, we are there through it all!

Distributed on a bi-monthly basis nationally, Nurture features comprehensive, informative feature articles giving their readers insight into the many styles of natural parenting that exist in today’s world, thus ‘allowing’ parents to take an ‘intuitive’ approach based on their own individual family’s needs.

Fundamentally, Nurture advocates the principles of Attachment Parenting and the Attachment Theory – A Secure Base for Babies and Parents. Attachment is a deep and enduring emotional bond that connects one person to another, across time and space (Ainsworth, 1973; Bowlby, 1969).

The parenting techniques and relational moments parents experience with their children can have a direct effect on the wiring of the brain and long-term nervous system regulation, as well as their capacity to have deeply enriching relationships with others. Given our current knowledge that some of the historically accepted ways of being with children can leave them vulnerable to suffering a range of mental, emotional, and physical health issues later in life, we no longer have the luxury of remaining ignorant about how society parents its children.

The attachments and challenges that we have with our own parents, our partners, our children, our siblings, and our friends can be strongly associated with the patterns of attachment blueprints we formed during the critical periods in our early development, with our primary caregiver. What parents do matters during this formative time (Anne Thistleton, 2018).
